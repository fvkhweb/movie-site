Three Generations, One Christmas 2026

Three Generations, One Christmas · Jody Margolin Hahn · комедия, драма, семейный

Set in a close-knit American town, The Starlight is a beloved local supper club and longtime community gathering place where generations have gathered to celebrate music, family, and Christmas traditions. Frank—a legendary song-and-dance man—prepares to sell the family’s beloved restaurant lounge after 40 years. With the future of The Starlight in question, three generations of family are drawn into an emotional journey that reminds them what is worth preserving — and how faith, family, and home can quietly lead us back to where we belong.
