RAYE brings her acclaimed album THIS MUSIC MAY CONTAIN HOPE. to the big screen in a breathtaking orchestral experience filmed at London’s Royal Albert Hall. Joined by 117 musicians, she reimagines the album in a powerful four-act journey through love, loss, faith, joy and the complexities of being human. Blending cinematic grandeur with intimate, heartfelt moments, this extraordinary performance celebrates family, resilience and the healing power of music.
