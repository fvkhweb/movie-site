What to Watch? is a fast, ad-free catalog of movies and TV series.

• Search across the whole catalog, filter by genre and year
• Curated collections: best of 2025, Korean cinema, thrillers for the evening, 90s classics
• Movie pages with cast, director, genres, ratings and fun facts
• Favorites — keep everything you plan to watch
• The catalog is updated daily and the app starts from a local snapshot, so it opens instantly even offline

No ads, no sign-up, no trackers. Open source under the MIT license.

The app interface is in Russian. Web version: https://fvkhweb.github.io/movie-site/
Source code: https://github.com/fvkhweb/movie-site
